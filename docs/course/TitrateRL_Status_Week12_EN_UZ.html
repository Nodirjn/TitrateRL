1. The project in one paragraph

People with Type 2 diabetes who start basal insulin (glargine) must adjust the daily dose for weeks until fasting glucose reaches the target. Today this is done with simple clinical rules (AT.LANTUS, Treat-to-Target, INSIGHT). We ask whether a reinforcement learning (RL) agent can titrate the dose better and as safely. We cannot test this on people, so we built a simulator of 150 virtual patients (Dalla Man T2D model with glargine absorption), trained PPO agents in it, and compared them with the clinical rules on 30 virtual patients the agents never saw.

2. What we measure and which methods we use

ItemContent
Main metricTIR: share of time with glucose 70–180 mg/dL in weeks 21–24 (maintenance phase), from noise-free plasma glucose. Higher is better.
Safety metricsTBR<70 and TBR<54: time below 70 and below 54 mg/dL (hypoglycemia). Lower is better. Consensus targets: TBR<70 < 4 %, TBR<54 < 1 %.
EnvironmentOne step = one day, 168 days (24 weeks). Action: change the dose by −4, −2, 0, +2 or +4 U. Hard limits for every policy: ≤ 8 U increase per 7 days, ≤ 1 U/kg.
AlgorithmPPO (Proximal Policy Optimization, Stable-Baselines3), 33 input features, 64×64 network.
RewardsR1 = TIR; R2 = negative glucose risk index; R3 = TIR − 5·TBR<70 − 10·TBR<54.
Safety shieldAfter a low (< 70) no dose increase; after a severe low (< 54) the dose is cut by 20 %.
ComparatorsB0 fixed dose, B1 AT.LANTUS, B2 Treat-to-Target, B3 INSIGHT.
StatisticsCommon random numbers (same meals and sensor noise for every policy), 95 % bootstrap CIs, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, Holm correction, Hodges–Lehmann effect sizes, Spearman correlation.

5. Results

5.1 System testing (Boburmirzo)

11 of 12 system tests pass. 92 unit and integration tests pass; one 24-week episode takes 0.20 s; same seed gives identical results; every policy sees the same meals; no policy ever exceeded +8 U per week or 1 U/kg; the shield works; all 21 trained agents load and run; the dashboard re-runs 3 policies × 24 weeks in 0.63 s. The only failure is ST-03 (check V4), kept failing on purpose because the criterion was fixed in advance.

5.2 Simulator validity, check V4 (Ibrohim)

V4 replays the Treat-to-Target clinical trial. In our main cohort, virtual patients do better than real ones (week-24 fasting glucose 99.6 vs 117 mg/dL). We tested 27 settings: a wider spread of insulin sensitivity does not close the gap, but missed injections do. With median sensitivity 3.0 and 15 % missed doses, V4 passes (fasting glucose 111.2 mg/dL, dose 0.53 U/kg, 32 % reach ≤ 100 mg/dL; trial: 117, 0.48, 36 %). Decision: keep the main cohort frozen, use this “calibrated world” as a robustness test.

5.3 Full training (Abdurashid)

20 runs: PPO with R1, R2, R3 (no shield) and R★ + shield, 5 seeds each, 10⁶ simulated days per run. The pre-registered rule (highest validation TIR among rewards where ≥ 90 % of patients meet the hypoglycemia targets) gave R★ = R1: R1 60.7 % / 97 %, R2 60.5 % / 93 %, R3 59.5 % / 100 %.

5.6 Robustness: patients who miss doses

In the calibrated world (more sensitive patients, 15 % missed injections, no retraining) the TIR advantage of RL disappears (gain over Treat-to-Target −0.1 pp). Agents without the shield leave 17–37 % of patients above the hypoglycemia targets; the shielded agent keeps all patients within them. The safety layer is essential.

Main message. RL improves time in range by 2–4 percentage points over guideline rules with very little hypoglycemia, but less than the 5 pp we set as clinically meaningful. When patients miss injections, the benefit vanishes and only the safety shield keeps dosing safe.

5. Natijalar

5.1 System testlar (Boburmirzo)

12 ta testdan 11 tasi o‘tdi. 92 ta unit va integratsion test o‘tadi; bitta 24 haftalik epizod 0,20 soniya oladi; bir xil seed bir xil natija beradi; har bir siyosat bir xil ovqatni ko‘radi; hech bir siyosat haftasiga +8 birlik yoki 1 birlik/kg chegarasidan oshmadi; shield ishlaydi; 21 ta o‘qitilgan agentning hammasi yuklanadi va ishlaydi; dashboard 3 ta siyosatni 24 hafta uchun 0,63 soniyada qayta hisoblaydi. 5.2 Simulyator to‘g‘riligi, V4 tekshiruvi (Ibrohim)

V4 Treat-to-Target klinik sinovini simulyatorda takrorlaydi. Asosiy kohortada virtual bemorlar haqiqiy bemorlardan yaxshiroq natija ko‘rsatadi (24-haftada och qoringa glyukoza 99,6 ga qarshi 117 mg/dL). 27 ta sozlamani sinadik: insulin sezgirligining tarqoqligini oshirish farqni yopmaydi, lekin ukollarni o‘tkazib yuborish yopadi. Sezgirlik medianasi 3,0 va ukollarning 15 % i o‘tkazib yuborilganda V4 o‘tadi (glyukoza 111,2 mg/dL, doza 0,53 birlik/kg, 32 % bemor ≤ 100 mg/dL ga yetadi; sinovda: 117, 0,48, 36 %). Qaror: asosiy kohorta o‘zgarmaydi, bu “kalibrlangan dunyo” robustlik testi sifatida ishlatiladi.

5.3 To‘liq o‘qitish (Abdurashid)

20 ta run: R1, R2, R3 bilan PPO (shield’siz) va R★ + shield, har biri 5 seed, har bir run 10⁶ simulyatsiya kuni. Oldindan ro‘yxatga olingan qoida (gipoglikemiya maqsadlarini bemorlarning ≥ 90 % i bajargan mukofotlar orasida validatsiya TIR eng yuqorisi) R★ = R1 ni berdi: R1 60,7 % / 97 %, R2 60,5 % / 93 %, R3 59,5 % / 100 %.

5.6 Robustlik: ukolni o‘tkazib yuboradigan bemorlar

Kalibrlangan dunyoda (sezgirroq bemorlar, ukollarning 15 % i o‘tkazib yuboriladi, agentlar qayta o‘qitilmagan) RL ning TIR bo‘yicha ustunligi yo‘qoladi (Treat-to-Target ga nisbatan −0,1 pp). Shield’siz agentlarda bemorlarning 17–37 % i gipoglikemiya maqsadlaridan chiqib ketadi; shield’li agent esa hamma bemorni maqsad ichida saqlaydi. Xavfsizlik qatlami zarur.

Asosiy xulosa. RL klinik qoidalarga nisbatan diapazondagi vaqtni 2–4 foiz punktga oshiradi va gipoglikemiya juda kam bo‘ladi, lekin bu biz klinik ahamiyatli deb belgilagan 5 pp dan kam. Bemorlar ukollarni o‘tkazib yuborganda ustunlik yo‘qoladi va faqat xavfsizlik shield’i dozalashni xavfsiz saqlaydi.
